Why It Matters

🚨

Urgent Crisis

Indonesia hosts 12% of the world's mammal species, but many face extinction due to habitat loss and poaching.

🎓

Education First

Schools and communities play a vital role in conservation through awareness, reporting, and active participation.

📸

Citizen Science

Your sighting reports help researchers track populations and protect critical habitats across Indonesia.

How It Works

Komodo Hub connects schools, communities, and citizens in conservation

🏫

Schools

Teachers create conservation programs and challenges. Students report sightings, complete learning materials, and track their progress — all integrated into the classroom.

🤝

Communities

Local communities join as groups, participate in conservation challenges, report species sightings from their region, and contribute to Indonesia's national wildlife database.

👤

General Public

Anyone can browse the species knowledge base and view sighting reports from across Indonesia — no account required. Join a school or community to contribute.
